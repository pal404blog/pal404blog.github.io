Here’s a fun exercise. Go open your banking app, look at your purchase history from late November, and ask yourself a simple question: *Did I actually save any money, or did I just fall for a digital magic trick?*

If you bought anything during Amazon’s endless Black Friday and **cyber** Monday extravaganza, brace yourself. A recent investigation by the Groundwork Collaborative dropped a truth bomb that should shock us, but honestly, just makes us look like a bunch of suckers. Nearly one in three—let me repeat that, *one in three*—deals advertised by the world’s biggest retailer offered absolutely zero savings. 

None. Zilch. Nada. 

In fact, almost one in ten items actually cost *more* during the sale than they did in the weeks leading up to it. 

Let that sink in. We are living in a golden age of algorithmic gaslighting.
